About the Event

The Fall Career Fair gives students the opportunity to meet employers from a variety of industries and learn about internships, full-time positions, and future career paths.

What to Expect

  • Meet employers from different industries.
  • Learn about internship opportunities.
  • Explore full-time career opportunities.
  • Connect with employers and other students.

Event Schedule

  • 11:00 AM - Doors open and student check-in begins.
  • 11:30 AM - Employer networking begins.
  • 1:00 PM - Career preparation session.
  • 2:30 PM - Final networking period.
  • 3:00 PM - Event concludes.

Accessibility and Participation

The event is open to all students. The Student Recreation Center has accessible entrances and seating. Students who need additional accommodations may contact the event organizers before attending.
